Highland Hole Out

Highland Hole Out is Birdeon's tartan collection — navy grounds crossed with purple and orange, a black tartan with neon pink and lime, and brighter women's colourways where the skirt is cut in the reverse of the polo. Each look is shown as a polo, bottom, cap and socks. Products will be available soon.
