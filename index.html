Golf apparel, coming soon

Colour on the course.

Birdeon is a new golf apparel label with three catalogues — tartan, winter layers and bright plain colour — for golfers in Australia, the Philippines and Taiwan.

Explore the catalogues

The brand

Tartan, colour, and a bird on the tag.

Birdeon is the golf apparel project of Damien Mueller and Edd Adair. The first three catalogues will be released individually: Highland Hole Out, the tartan set; Winter League, a darker collection with men's golf trousers; and Summer Links, bright coral and aqua in plain colour.

Pieces are designed as looks — a polo, a short or skirt, a cap and socks — with a small leather chest tag on the shirts and a printed full-colour logo on the golf bag. Nothing is for sale yet. Fabric details are still to come, and every product on this site is marked coming soon.
