About

A new name on the bag.

Birdeon is a golf apparel brand started by Damien Mueller and Edd Adair. The work so far is visual — catalogues of men's and women's golf clothing, still on their way to the shop floor.

Who is behind Birdeon?

Damien Mueller, based in Australia, and Edd Adair lead the project together. The brand name is Birdeon. The first pieces are being designed as three catalogues, to be released individually rather than as a single drop.

What is coming?

Highland Hole Out is the tartan collection: men's golf polos and shorts, women's polos with reverse-colour tartan golf skirts, leather-patch caps, and a navy tartan golf bag with the printed full-colour logo.

Winter League goes darker, with men's charcoal golf trousers and a forest quarter-zip, plus a women's burgundy and forest tartan set that keeps a skirt.

Summer Links is bright and plain — coral and aqua, without tartan — for warm-weather golf.

The clothes are being designed with golfers in Australia, the Philippines and Taiwan in mind. Fabric details, sizes and prices will follow. Nothing on this site is for sale yet.
